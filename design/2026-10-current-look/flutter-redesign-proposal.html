What changes in the design

Departures board first

The next deadline is the whole top of the app.

Split-flap countdown, red used only for deadlines, so a red item always means something is due.

One timeline, many lists

Doctor, school and home stop living in separate places.

TickTick lists become filter chips. Calendar events and tasks share one week view with free blocks visible.

Native timer

Survives locking the phone.

Lock-screen and watch timer, ending notification with Done and +5 min actions, instead of a web page that sleeps.

Offline first

Deadlines show on the train.

Cache the board locally; queue Done taps and XP rows and send them when back online.

Feature ideas for your rules

One-tap sync check

Your rule: Notion is the archive, TickTick is the view.

A "Sync" screen that lists anything dated in Notion but missing in TickTick (or the reverse), with a Fix button. It would have caught the Sep 29 homework.

Add a deadline by typing or voice

Docs stays a place you can dump into.

"Chem lab report Friday 11:59" creates the Notion row and the TickTick task together. Dump a paragraph from Docs and it asks only about the dates it cannot read.

Overdue triage

Seven past-due items is a lot to look at.

A short flow: finish, reschedule or drop each. It reschedules into your free calendar blocks instead of piling onto today.

Study plan from an exam date

Exams are the deadlines that need lead time.

Pick an exam and it drafts spaced review blocks (Chem, Math, English colours) into the week, which you accept or edit.

Weight-aware priority

Notion already stores Weight % and scores.

Rank the board by how much a task moves your grade, not only by date. Show the running course grade beside each course.

Gentle streaks and Sunday review

You already plan Sundays at 6 PM.

A weekly recap: blocks done, hours missed, next week's heavy days, then a prefilled plan. Streaks pause on rest days instead of breaking.

Health and errands lane

Your doctor list is long and undated.

Undated items get a "book by" nudge and a call-hours reminder. Stay separate from school deadlines so they never crowd the countdown.

Home-screen widgets

Seeing the next deadline should cost zero taps.

Small: countdown. Medium: next three. Lock screen: days left to the next exam.

How it would be built

Native Flutter screens call the same Cloudflare Worker the website uses, so sign-in, TickTick and sheets keep working and the site stays as the desktop version. Nothing personal goes in the repo.

app/lib/
  theme/        tokens (light, dark), Barlow Condensed + Plex Mono
  data/         worker client, local cache, offline queue
  features/
    today/      flap countdown, overdue list
    board/      week timeline, filters, swipe actions
    focus/      dial, XP, block rating, notifications
    quests/     XP log, streaks
    sync/       Notion vs TickTick check

Suggested order: 1) theme and Today screen, 2) Board, 3) Focus with notifications, 4) Sync check. Each can ship as its own pull request, and the web view stays as a fallback until a screen is replaced.
